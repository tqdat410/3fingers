# 3fingers

A compact workspace for reading several social feeds in desktop Chrome.

## Brand

- Primary: charcoal `#1C1C1C` (`--brand-primary`), sampled from the official logo. Light mode uses white surface, `#F7F7F7` canvas, `#686868` muted text and `#E5E5E5` borders. Dark mode uses `#1C1C1C` surface, `#242424` canvas, `#F1F1F1` text, `#AAAAAA` muted text and `#393939` borders. Focus and icons follow text contrast; errors retain their own color.
- Font: bundled, unmodified Ubuntu 400/500/700 TTF from Google Fonts under UFL 1.0. System fonts cover missing glyphs, including Vietnamese. No font requests or font changes inside social pages.
- Logo: original 1254 x 1254 RGBA PNG at `extension/assets/brand/logo-original.png`. Icons at 16/32/48/128 crop the visible area with padding and preserve transparency. Header, favicon and extension action share the logo. The popup's dark variant renders the existing mark white while preserving alpha, with no background badge or border; light mode retains the charcoal mark.
- Title: `3fingers`. Extension description: `Read social feeds side by side with synced scrolling.` Fixed quote: `Multitask is not real!` Preserve the quote's exact wording, capitalization and punctuation. Show it once below the brand in the popup, with no repeated quote in the title/header, and in the README. All extension labels, tooltips, status messages, errors and accessibility text use short English. Localized site matching and multilingual test data remain functional data.

## Layout and controls

The Go button is temporarily hidden. Its markup, style and click handler remain available for restoration; it takes no space and cannot receive keyboard focus while hidden.

- Up to three panels. One fills the workspace; three use equal widths. With two, the width picker displays 33%, 50% and 66%, using exact complementary thirds/halves underneath. The keyed split follows its panel through reorder, persists on reload and resets when panel count leaves two. Preserve iframe documents when changing widths; retain a 280px minimum per panel with horizontal overflow on narrow screens. New workspaces show Facebook, Threads and Instagram with an Open button. Older surplus panels are saved for restoration.
- No main toolbar in the workspace or vertical separators between social panels. Outside the workspace, the Chrome action opens or focuses the workspace directly, including its window when needed. In the workspace, the action opens a 300px popup. Its logo/name/quote group aligns to the left padding. A compact 44px square Open workspace button on the right echoes the logo’s rounded silhouette, with an inverted text/surface palette and oversized bold 48px `Go` lettering, optically centered in both axes and cropped by the rounded silhouette to echo the logo’s cutout detail. Hover softens the fill; pressing scales the button slightly. Reduced motion removes these movements. No setup hint is shown when the workspace is closed. GitHub link, tool light/dark, Reload all and + remain below. GitHub opens https://github.com/tqdat410/3fingers. Opening the workspace focuses an existing tab or creates one. Controls target the active workspace in the current window when available. Reload all starts all permitted panels together; it is disabled when no panels are enabled. + is disabled at three sites. An empty workspace points to the extension menu. Popup content has no outer border; Chrome draws its own native bubble frame outside the page.
- Panel toolbar: 32px, Back then Reload and site sun/moon on the left, supplied pin icon centered at 18px, width picker then change-site and close on the right. Buttons are 28px with tooltips, keyboard focus and accessible names. Back is disabled without panel-local history; width is disabled outside a two-panel layout. The width picker is anchored to its button and supports arrow keys, Home, End, Enter, Escape and outside dismissal. No management menu, free resize controls or separate site-name bar.
- Panel site picker: anchored to its button, with Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Reddit and YouTube Shorts. No title, footer or backdrop. Supports outside dismissal, Escape, arrow keys, Home, End and Enter. Stays within the viewport. The popup's + reveals the same social choices and any saved sites for restoration. Duplicate sites are allowed.
- Site and width dropdowns close on unrelated outside click, wheel or focus changes, including pointer, wheel, touch and key input in any social feed. Menu-internal interaction keeps the menu open. Dismissal does not steal focus from a feed or block its input. Frame notifications are subscribed only while an extension dropdown is open.
- Click the pin to toggle independent scrolling. Outline joins sync; solid excludes the panel as both source and follower. Enter/Space toggle; arrow keys reorder. Pin updates serialize configuration without applying disabled-opacity styling to every topbar button. Pin preferences stay with the panel through reload, reorder and site replacement. TikTok and YouTube always use native independent scrolling: show a solid pin with aria-disabled=true, preserve its keyboard reorder, and make pin clicks a no-op. Do not overwrite its stored pin preference; replacing either with another social restores that preference. There is no global sync toggle; rejoining keeps the current reading position.
- Drag empty space in the panel topbar at least 6px to reorder. Buttons and their icons never start dragging; the pin is click-only for pointer input. The panel scales to 0.96 and follows the pointer in both axes while showing its new position. Release saves; Escape cancels. Drag, cancel and keyboard ordering never toggle the pin. A single panel still supports pinning. Preserve iframe viewport, document and scroll offset without reloading.

## Scrolling and site content

- Reddit uses pin-controlled feed sync. Opening it requests optional cookies access plus exact www/parent Reddit hosts for a read-only csrf_token fallback in registered direct documents. Preserve native cookie attributes/setters and nonempty values; refresh on cookie changes, disable on revocation and never persist/log tokens. YouTube supports Shorts only and opens /shorts/; full-site panels and archived entries migrate there while existing Shorts links and panel preferences remain. Shorts scrolls independently, with fullscreen delegated only to its exact www.youtube.com frame. Native links stay visible; regular video/search navigation is outside support and is not intercepted. Local drag uses the same temporary snap suppression as TikTok. Reddit and Shorts theme controls select verified native palette flags per panel without changing site preferences. Existing signed-in sessions can be reused; cross-origin sign-in stays in normal site tabs. X opens its native reading feed with narrow x.com access plus optional cookies for fixed ct0 reads. Its theme control uses native Light/Lights out radios on the dedicated Display route, then restores the frame route/history without reload; the native screen can appear briefly. It joins generic sync/pin and local drag; extension-driven URLs and Back preserve the cache-exclusion lang parameter without changing existing language values. Pre-existing api.x.com/broad grants show a panel-local compatibility error rather than being silently revoked. Auxiliary API/secondary-feature failures remain a Beta limitation.
- TikTok keeps native wheel, touch, keyboard, playback, comments and Auto Scroll behavior. Mouse/pen press-and-drag scrolls its feed independently using the shared gesture threshold and control exclusions. Suspend video snapping only during the active drag; restore the original scroll-snap-type value and priority on release or cancellation. Its movements never synchronize other feeds, and other feeds never move it. No custom auto-next or prefetch is added. Its theme button activates the site's own More > Dark/Light control, including its complete native palette and preference behavior. Conceal the automatically opened drawer until it finishes closing, then remove temporary styling; never conceal or close a menu the user already opened. Do not force root attributes or add color filters. Observe actual native choices so the toolbar follows changes made inside TikTok.
- Back returns only the selected panel to its previous recorded same-origin URL. It can reload that page; it does not reproduce arbitrary in-memory SPA state or drafts. Browser joint-history traversal can rewind sibling frames, so the extension replaces only the selected frame's location. A bounded route list lives in the existing runtime session and clears when the panel is removed or changes site.
- Dragging feed content scrolls that feed independently, including TikTok. A movement threshold preserves normal clicks. Inputs, small buttons, video controls, dialogs and nested scrollers keep native behavior. Shift allows text selection. Native touch scrolling stays local; wheel and keyboard sync only eligible unpinned panels.
- Comments and dialogs scroll independently and pause their background feed. Sync resumes when the dialog closes. Connection and permission states remain distinct from a loaded site.
- Appending a page below the viewport preserves an active synchronized wheel gesture. Layout anchoring, range clamping, root replacement and viewport changes reset the scroll baseline to avoid sending automatic movement to peers. Shrinking below the viewport preserves user movement; layout resets retain remaining wheel intent unless the scroll root changes.
- Keep native navigation, posts, inputs, cards, media and comments. Theme controls use existing native light/dark palettes without media filters. Meta, Reddit and Shorts choices persist per panel; TikTok and X own their native preferences and may share them with other tabs of that social. Only TikTok's three native theme-cookie writes receive embedded-compatible SameSite/Secure attributes when no SameSite was specified; other cookies and browser policy stay untouched. Unsupported structures keep native presentation. The separate tool theme affects extension components only. Hide only feed scrollbars. During a swipe, temporarily change the cursor and suppress selection. Do not crop, reparent or rebuild feeds or hide site controls. Compact reading mode remains out of scope.
- Site names and domains remain available in tooltips, accessible labels, the picker and unopened panels. No user content enters product analytics or logs.
- Controls remain reachable in narrow columns; desktop is the primary target. Respect reduced motion. Do not replace the layout with Chrome's two-tab split.

This is a local extension UI. SEO, sitemaps, AI discovery and sharing integrations are outside its scope.
